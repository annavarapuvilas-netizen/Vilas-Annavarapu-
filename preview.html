Riverside Collective

A worker-owned coffee shop and ice cream bar in South Jackson, Mississippi, run by the students who work there.

Riverside began in a middle school classroom. As a Mississippi Teacher Corps fellow at Blackburn Middle School, I saw my students struggle to work as a team, and I fell in love with teaching them. I wanted to build a place where young people could practice working together and be paid fairly for it.

So we built one. Riverside Collective is a nonprofit that turned an abandoned bank building into a coffeehouse and community hub, where young people run the business, earn a wage, and vote on how it operates and how profits are shared.

I raised more than $200,000 to open the doors, designed the ownership structure, financial models and accounting systems, and built the entrepreneurship curriculum that teaches students to run and co-own a business as a way to build wealth. The long-term goal is for Riverside to become an incubator for other worker-owned businesses.
