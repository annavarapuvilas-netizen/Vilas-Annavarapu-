About

I founded and led a worker-owned cooperative, embedded environmental justice and workforce-development principles into state carbon and clean energy policy, and taught in a Title I school. Across all of it, I convene developers, utilities, labor unions and school districts to win durable local buy-in.

Work

Environmental Justice Fellow

Jan 2024 – Aug 2025

Great Plains Institute · Jackson, MS

  • Unified 30+ community-based organizations to develop Mississippi's first Just Transition plan, mapping pathways for local ownership of clean energy and the jobs it creates.
  • Wrote the environmental justice and workforce provisions of the Carbon Management Roadmaps adopted by the Colorado and Texas legislatures.
  • Built workforce pipelines with developers, unions and school districts, including a welding and carbon-management training partnership in Vernon Parish, Louisiana.
  • Designed the Carbon Action Alliance's Civic Toolkit for community benefit agreements and equitable stakeholder engagement.

Co-Founder & President

Nov 2021 – Aug 2025

Riverside Collective · Jackson, MS

  • Raised $200,000+ to convert an abandoned bank building into a worker-owned coffeehouse and community hub, with student workers as co-owners.
  • Designed the ownership structure, financial models and accounting systems so worker-owners share both accountability and upside.
  • Built a student-entrepreneurship curriculum teaching high schoolers to run and co-own a business as a wealth-building strategy in South Jackson.
  • Selected for the Annie E. Casey Foundation's inaugural Rising Leader for Results Fellowship.
